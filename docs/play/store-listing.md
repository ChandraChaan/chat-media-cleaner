## Full description (4000 max)

```
Chat Media Cleaner is a file manager for your whole phone, with a cleaner for the WhatsApp photos, videos and voice notes that quietly fill it up. Everything happens on your phone: the app has no internet permission, no account and no ads.

FILE MANAGER
• Browse internal storage and your memory card, folder by folder, with a path bar to jump back up
• Copy, move, rename and delete files and whole folders
• Create folders, select many items at once, share them or open them in another app
• Sort by name, date or size, filter by kind of file, and search a folder and everything below it
• Compress files and folders into a ZIP, and extract ZIP archives
• Favourites for the folders and files you use most, and your recently opened files
• Details for any file or folder: size, what it contains, dates, pixel size and length
• Show or hide hidden files

SEE WHERE YOUR SPACE GOES
• Storage use for each volume
• Space taken by images, videos, audio, documents, archives and apps (APK)
• Large files, biggest first
• Exact duplicates anywhere on your phone (same content, checked with SHA-256); the oldest copy is always kept

CLEAN UP CHAT MEDIA
• Finds your WhatsApp and WhatsApp Business media folders for you
• Groups what you probably don't need: huge videos, the same file saved many times, old forwards and memes, and copies of media you sent
• You choose what goes; nothing is pre-selected

OPEN FILES RIGHT HERE
• Photos and GIFs with pinch to zoom
• A full video player: swipe for brightness and volume, double-tap to skip, speed, lock, sleep timer, save a frame
• Voice notes and music, PDFs page by page, and text files

SAFE BY DESIGN
• Deleted files go to the app's trash first and can be restored for 7 days (1 to 30, your choice), with Undo right after deleting
• Nothing is deleted until you select it and confirm

ABOUT "ALL FILES ACCESS"
To work as a file manager across your whole storage, the app asks for Android's "All files access". It is only used to show, open, copy, move, compress and delete the files you choose. You can say no: the app then works in the folders you pick with Android's folder picker.

Chat Media Cleaner is an independent app. It is not made by, affiliated with or endorsed by WhatsApp or Meta.
```
